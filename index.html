1. Upload the brand logo

Flat, solid-color logos trace best.
The background is removed automatically. Transparent PNGs and logos on white or a plain color both work.
Each solid color becomes its own shape group, so the colored version keeps the brand palette.
Gradients and photographs can't be traced cleanly. Ask the partner for a flat or vector logo instead.
What you getA copy of the kiosk screensaver with the brand logo in the intro, the header of each scene, and the outro.
Two logo versionsA colored version for the light scenes and a white version that fades in over the dark "Transparent Pricing" scene.
Where it goesDownload the .json and drop it into the Drive folder ATP - Marketing › ATP Screensaver › English version next to the other brands.
